✨ Action & Event Symbols

x
Capture (吃子)

Indicates a piece took an enemy piece. Example: Bxf7 (Bishop captures on f7), exd5 (e-pawn captures on d5).

+
Check (將軍)

Appended to the move when the opponent's King is under direct attack. Example: Qh5+.

#
Checkmate (將死/將殺)

Marks the final winning move! (Sometimes written as ++). Example: Qxf7#.

O-O
Kingside Castling (短易位)

Two capital letters O with a hyphen. Short castling toward the h-file.

O-O-O
Queenside Castling (長易位)

Three capital letters O with hyphens. Long castling toward the a-file.

=Q
Promotion (兵的升變)

An equals sign followed by the promoted piece letter. Example: e8=Q or c8=N.

❓ Disambiguation: Which Piece Moved? (消歧義)

If two identical pieces (like two Knights or two Rooks) can both legally move to the exact same destination square, we specify the starting file (column) or starting rank (row) of the piece that moved.

Nbd2

"The Knight that was on the b-file moves to d2" (to distinguish it from a Knight on the f-file that could also move to d2).

R1e2

"The Rook that was on the 1st rank moves to e2" (to distinguish from a Rook on the 8th rank on the same file).

🏆 Match Results & Tournament Scoring

How do chess matches end and how are scores tallied in a club or tournament setting? Here is the universal standard used everywhere from local clubs to the World Chess Championship!

1 - 0

White Wins (白勝)

White checkmates Black, Black runs out of time on their clock, or Black resigns (surrenders).

White: 1 pt | Black: 0 pt
½ - ½

Draw / Tie (和棋)

Stalemate, threefold repetition, 50-move rule, insufficient material, or mutual agreement.

White: ½ pt | Black: ½ pt
0 - 1

Black Wins (黑勝)

Black checkmates White, White runs out of time on their clock, or White resigns.

White: 0 pt | Black: 1 pt

⚖️ Understanding Tournament Tie-Breaks

When two or more players finish a tournament with the exact same point total, tie-break systems determine final placements:

Buchholz System (布赫茲系數)

Calculates the sum of all your opponents' total scores. Players who faced tougher, higher-scoring opponents receive a higher tie-break rank!

Direct Encounter (勝負關係)

If the tied players played each other in an earlier round of the tournament, whoever won that head-to-head game takes the higher spot.
